1. Refund Policy

We want you to have a smooth shopping experience. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, you may request a refund under the conditions below.

Eligibility for Refund

Customers can request a refund within 7 days from the date of delivery.

Refund requests are accepted when:

Product is damaged upon arrival
Wrong item was delivered
Product has manufacturing defects
Item is missing from the package
Product quality significantly differs from description
Refund Not Eligible For

Refunds may not be approved if:

Request is made after 7 days of delivery
Product has been used excessively
Item is damaged by customer misuse
Product is missing original packaging
Digital products or downloadable items
Personalized/custom-made products
Refund Process
Open "My Orders"
Select the order
Click "Request Refund"
Choose reason
Upload images if required
Submit request

Our support team will review the request within 2–3 business days.

Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed within:

UPI: 2–5 business days
Credit/Debit Card: 5–7 business days
Net Banking: 5–7 business days
Wallet: 1–3 business days

Refunds will be sent to the original payment method.

2. Return Policy
Return Window

Products can be returned within 7 days of delivery.

Return Conditions

Returned products must:

Be unused
Include original tags
Include original packaging
Include accessories and invoices
Return Pickup

Depending on your location:

Doorstep pickup may be available
Self-shipping may be required

If self-shipping is required and approved, shipping charges may be reimbursed.

3. Cancellation Policy

Orders can be cancelled before shipment.

After shipment:

Cancellation may not be available
Customer can create a return request after delivery if eligible

Refund for cancelled orders:

Prepaid orders → refunded automatically
COD orders → bank details may be requested

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I get a refund after 7 days?
Refund requests after 7 days are generally not accepted.

Q: How long do refunds take?
Usually 2–7 business days after approval.

Q: Can I return opened products?
Yes, if they meet return eligibility requirements.

Q: Can I cancel after shipping?
Usually no; return options may be available after delivery.

Q: How do I track my order?
Use your Order ID in the "Track Order" section.
